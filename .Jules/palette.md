## 2026-07-22 - MainView Operation Splitter Tooltips and ARIA Labels\n**Learning:** HyperlinkButtons used for layout operations (like expanding/collapsing or showing menus) often lack tooltips and proper ARIA labels, making them inaccessible to screen readers and difficult to understand for sighted users when they only contain icons. Adding `ToolTipService.ToolTip` provides visual hints, while `AutomationProperties.Name` ensures accessibility.\n**Action:** Add `ToolTipService.ToolTip` and `AutomationProperties.Name` to the `OperationSplitterMenuButton` and `ExpandCollapseOpList` in `MainView.xaml`, translating their names in the code-behind to support localization.
